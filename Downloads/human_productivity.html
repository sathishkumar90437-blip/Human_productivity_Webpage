The human approach

Six habits that make
a real difference.

Productivity isn't about squeezing more into your day. It's about creating a day that works for you.

🧭

Pick your one thing

Choose the single task that would make today feel meaningful. Start there before the noise takes over.

⏳

Work in focused sprints

Try 25 minutes of distraction-free work, then take a 5-minute break. Repeat at a pace that feels right.

📵

Make distractions harder

Silence non-essential notifications, close extra tabs, and keep your phone out of reach while focusing.

📝

Get it out of your head

Write down tasks and ideas. A simple list frees up mental space and helps you decide what comes next.

🚶

Move to reset

Stretch, take a short walk, drink water, or look outside. A tiny reset can help you return with fresh energy.

🌿

Finish with reflection

Notice what worked, celebrate one small win, and choose a first step for tomorrow. Then let the day end.

Your little plan

Make space for what matters.

Add a few priorities for today. Keep it realistic—three meaningful tasks are better than a list that overwhelms you.
